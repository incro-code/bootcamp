The programme

Four weeks. Landscape, context, build, sustain. Each company gets a customised programme — the structure below is the default; we adapt scope, pace, and focus to your team before kick-off.

Week 1
Landscape & Workflow Map
Understanding the Claude ecosystem — and where your workflows fit
We run a live showcase of the full Claude infrastructure: Claude.ai, Claude API, Claude Code, Cowork, and connected tools. Then, using our proprietary Workflow-AI Matrix, we map your team's actual processes and help you decide which Claude is best for which task — and where AI should not be trusted unsupervised.
  • Claude tool showcase: what's in the ecosystem, what each tool is best for
  • Setting up Claude accounts optimised for finance-specific tasks
  • Pre-session AI readiness assessment sent to every team member
  • Workflow-AI Matrix: priority mapping across your team's processes
  • Clear boundaries: where Claude adds value, where human sign-off stays essential
Week 2
Finance Context & Skills
Building business and finance context so Claude actually understands your company
Raw AI is generic. This week is about making Claude fluent in your business — your chart of accounts, your KPI definitions, your reporting logic, your sector. We implement finance-specific skills and a structured business context layer so every prompt your team writes benefits from institutional knowledge that doesn't have to be re-explained every time.
  • Which skills to build for a finance team (variance analysis, board pack, close commentary)
  • Building your company's business context — sector, model, terminology, KPI definitions
  • Prompt engineering for finance: precision, auditability, reproducibility
  • Knowledge library handover: prompt templates, workflow specs, best practices
Week 3
Build Week — Live Agents
Every participant leaves with a working agent
This is the hands-on build workshop. Using Claude Cowork or Claude Code (matched to each participant's technical level), the team builds actual, deployable agents against their own workflows. No demos. No hypotheticals. Working software that runs on day one after the workshop.
  • Facilitated build workshop: Claude Cowork (no-code) and Claude Code (developer path)
  • Suggested agents: Variance Analyst, Close Commentary Generator, KPI Monitor, Cash Forecast Roller
  • Each participant or sub-team scopes, builds, and deploys their own agent
  • Dedicated support channel active throughout the programme for async questions
Week 4
Sustain & Scale
Lessons learned, fine-tuning, and building the culture that keeps going
The last week is about turning a 30-day sprint into a permanent operating mode. We review what worked, fine-tune agents based on real usage, and establish the governance and data architecture needed to move from Copilot mode to autonomous operation. Teams leave with a repeatable process for finding and implementing new AI workflows independently.
  • Agent performance review and fine-tuning session
  • Data architecture roadmap: what you need to make agents truly autonomous (data lakes, connections)
  • AI culture playbook: how to keep finding new use cases after we leave
  • Session recordings and full knowledge library handed over to the team

After the bootcamp

The programme ends. The infrastructure doesn't have to. For teams that want to go further, we offer three follow-on services.

Agent audit
Review & optimise what you built

We audit the agents your team deployed — reviewing prompt logic, output quality, and edge-case handling. You get a written assessment and a prioritised improvement plan.

Advanced build
Build more complex agents with us

For workflows that go beyond what the bootcamp covers — multi-step orchestration, external system integrations, approval workflows — we build alongside your team or on your behalf.

Data infrastructure
Build the data layer that makes agents powerful

Agents are only as good as the data they access. We build the data architecture — lakes, warehouses, semantic layers — that enables your agents to operate autonomously and your BI to connect directly to Claude.
